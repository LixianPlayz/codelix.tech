What Is a Business Email?

A business email uses your domain name instead of a free email provider. For example:

contact@yourbusiness.com

It looks more professional than something like yourbusiness@gmail.com.

How to Create a Business Email

  1. Buy a domain name, like yourbusiness.com.
  2. Choose an email host, such as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho Mail, or your web host.
  3. Create email accounts like support@, contact@, or sales@.
  4. Update your domain DNS records using the instructions from your email provider.
  5. Test sending and receiving emails.

How to Add Your Email to a Website

You can add a clickable email link using this HTML:

<a href="mailto:contact@yourbusiness.com">contact@yourbusiness.com</a>

Example button:

Email Us

You can also place your email in your footer, contact page, or support section.

How to Avoid Spam

Posting your email directly on a website can attract spam bots. Safer options include:

  • Use a contact form instead of showing your email directly.
  • Write your email like contact [at] yourbusiness [dot] com.
  • Use JavaScript to reveal the email only when a user clicks a button.
  • Use spam filters from your email provider.
